CLARION
Shows your PlayStation (or any console) full screen on your Mac, sharper and smoother.
Works with an HDMI capture card, or with the PS Remote Play app.

Needs: a Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Best on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).


1. INSTALL
   Drag Clarion into your Applications folder.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
   Clarion isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected.

   a. Double-click Clarion. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
   b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   c. Scroll down. Next to "Clarion was blocked", click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
   d. Click Open. From now on it opens normally.


3. PICK WHERE THE PICTURE COMES FROM
   Clarion opens full screen. Press Tab to open its settings, and under Source choose:

   - Capture card: for an HDMI capture card plugged into your Mac.
     Allow Camera (the video) and Microphone (the game sound) when asked.

   - Remote Play app: for the PS Remote Play app on this Mac (no capture card needed).
     Start streaming in PS Remote Play first. Allow Screen Recording when asked,
     then click Quit & Reopen. Clarion finds the Remote Play window by itself.
     Keep PS Remote Play open; it can sit behind Clarion. Game sound comes from Remote Play.


4. KEYS
   Tab   settings
   O     stats: off / compact / full
   U     sharper upscaling on / off
   S     sharpening on / off
   F     smooth 60 fps on / off (macOS 26 or newer; adds about 0.04 s of delay, so leave it off for fast games)
   V     V-Sync on / off (off = less delay, may tear)
   M     fit / fill / stretch
   P     take a screenshot (saved in Pictures > Clarion)
   R     start / stop recording, with sound (saved in Movies > Clarion)
   ⌘Q    quit


5. TIPS
   - For the least delay, leave F off and connect your PlayStation and Mac by ethernet cable.
   - If the picture freezes in Remote Play mode, move the PS Remote Play window to another screen.
   - If something says access is off: System Settings > Privacy & Security, find the right
     section (Camera, Microphone or Screen & System Audio Recording), turn Clarion on, and reopen it.
